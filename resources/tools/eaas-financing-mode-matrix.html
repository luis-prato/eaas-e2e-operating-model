Financing Mode Overview
Purchase
Asset Owner: Customer
Revenue: One-time sale
Financing
Asset Owner: Customer (post-term)
Revenue: Installments + interest
Leasing
Asset Owner: Provider / Lessor
Revenue: Periodic lease payments
Outsourcing
Asset Owner: Provider
Revenue: Service fee (outcome-neutral)
AaaS
Asset Owner: Provider + Bank
Revenue: Usage / performance fee
Scoring Instructions
ADL Benchmark — Pre-filled reference scores (1–5) from the ADL framework showing how well each financing mode fits each criterion in general.
Your Weight — How important is this criterion for your situation? (1 = low, 2 = medium, 3 = high importance)
Your Score — Override the ADL benchmark with your own score (1–5) based on your specific context.
The Weighted Total per mode is calculated as: Σ (Your Weight × Your Score) across all 6 criteria. Higher total = stronger fit.
Score 1 = Very weak fit Score 2 = Weak fit Score 3 = Neutral Score 4 = Good fit Score 5 = Strong fit | Weight 1 = Low importance Weight 2 = Medium importance Weight 3 = High importance
