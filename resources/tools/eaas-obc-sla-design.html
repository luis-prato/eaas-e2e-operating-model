SLA Design Principles
Baseline first — All targets must reference a measured baseline. Never set targets without measurement data.  |  Measurement before contract — Agree on verification method before signing. Measurement disputes are the #1 source of EaaS contract failure.  |  Bonus/penalty symmetry — If the provider can earn a bonus, they should bear a proportional penalty.  |  Force majeure carve-outs — Exclude customer-caused downtime, planned shutdowns, and external supply disruptions.  |  Remedy ladder — Service credit first → corrective action plan → termination right for persistent failure.
