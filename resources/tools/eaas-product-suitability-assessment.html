Step 1A — Customer Lens Assessment

Rate your equipment from your customer's perspective. How do customers experience it today?

# Characteristic What it means Score (0–3)
1 High purchase price The upfront capital cost is a significant barrier — typical for heavy equipment, robotics, or specialised hardware
2 Predictable usage Customers use the equipment in regular, foreseeable production or operational cycles
3 High maintenance or operating costs Customers face significant ongoing costs for servicing, calibration, parts, or consumables
4 Long useful life / durability The equipment lasts many years when properly maintained — typical of industrial machinery and professional hardware
5 Upgradeable or modular The equipment or robotics system can be upgraded, reconfigured, or expanded over time
6 Performance outcomes matter most Customers care about uptime, throughput, accuracy, or output — not owning the hardware itself
7 Customers lack technical expertise Customers struggle with maintenance, calibration, software updates, or skilled operation of the equipment
8 High residual value The equipment retains significant market or functional value after the customer's use period

Step 1B — Provider Lens Assessment

Rate your equipment from your own perspective as the provider. Are you positioned to deliver and operate EaaS?

# Characteristic What it means Score (0–3)
1 You know how customers use the equipment You have data or direct insight into usage patterns, shift hours, load cycles, or operational conditions
2 You can monitor equipment condition remotely Sensors, telematics, IoT connectivity, or diagnostic systems let you track equipment health and usage
3 You can maintain or service the equipment efficiently You have service technicians, authorised repair networks, or field service capabilities
4 You can recover and reuse the equipment at end-of-use Take-back logistics and refurbishment processes exist or are feasible for your equipment type
5 You have or can build recurring customer relationships You're equipped to manage multi-year service contracts, SLAs, and subscription billing
6 You have equipment cost clarity You understand the true lifetime cost of ownership per unit — including service, parts, and depreciation
7 Your equipment has high reuse or remanufacturing potential Returned hardware or robotics systems can be refurbished and redeployed profitably
8 You can absorb upfront investment and wait for returns Your business can fund equipment deployment now and recover cost through service fees over time
