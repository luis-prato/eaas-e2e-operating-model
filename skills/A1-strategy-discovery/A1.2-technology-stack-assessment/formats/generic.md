# A1.2 — Technology Stack Assessment

> **EaaS E2E Operating Model** · Group: A1 · Strategy & Discovery · Role: Technology Lead
> Version: 1.24.0 · License: Apache-2.0 · Author: Luis Prato

**Summary:** Evaluate your current technology systems against EaaS requirements and build a roadmap to close capability gaps before your first contract.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Technology capability gap analysis
- Build vs buy vs integrate decision framework
- Stack integration architecture
- Technology investment roadmap

---

## What This Skill Covers

Technology Stack Assessment evaluates the complete technology landscape — hardware, software, connectivity, and integration layers — to determine whether the existing stack can support an EaaS / outcome-based business model, and what investment is required to close gaps.

---

## Why It Matters in EaaS

EaaS requires a technology stack that can: (1) generate reliable operational data, (2) enable remote monitoring and diagnostics, (3) support automated billing and contract management, and (4) scale across a fleet of assets. Gaps in any layer create operational risk that gets priced into contracts — or causes disputes.

---

## Key Activities

1. **Stack inventory** — Document all technology layers: sensors, controllers (PLCs/CNCs), edge compute, connectivity (4G/5G/Wi-Fi), cloud platforms, applications (ERP, MES, CMMS, CRM)
2. **EaaS readiness scoring** — Score each layer against EaaS requirements: remote access, real-time telemetry, OTA updates, fleet management
3. **Vendor dependency mapping** — Identify proprietary lock-in, end-of-life components, and single points of failure
4. **Cybersecurity posture review** — Assess OT/IT security maturity for always-on, internet-connected asset operation
5. **Integration complexity estimate** — Quantify effort to connect existing stack to EaaS management platform
6. **Build vs. buy vs. partner decision** — Evaluate whether gaps are filled internally, through commercial platforms, or via partnerships

---

## Assessment Framework: EaaS Technology Readiness Levels (TRL)

| Level | Description | Action |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| TRL 1 | No connectivity, no telemetry | Major investment required before EaaS |
| TRL 2 | Basic connectivity, manual data collection | Retrofit IoT layer; 6–18 month roadmap |
| TRL 3 | Automated telemetry, limited analytics | Integrate analytics and alerting layer |
| TRL 4 | Real-time monitoring, basic remote diagnostics | Enhance fleet management and billing |
| TRL 5 | Full remote control, predictive analytics, automated billing | EaaS-ready; focus on optimisation |

## Key Outputs

- Technology Stack Map (current state)
- EaaS Technology Readiness Score (per layer)
