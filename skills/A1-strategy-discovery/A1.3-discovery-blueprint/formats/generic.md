# A1.3 — Discovery Blueprint

> **EaaS E2E Operating Model** · Group: A1 · Strategy & Discovery · Role: Strategy Lead
> Version: 1.24.0 · License: Apache-2.0 · Author: Luis Prato

**Summary:** Structure the initial customer discovery phase to understand operational context, pain points, and outcome ambitions before any solution is proposed.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Customer discovery interview guide
- Operational baseline documentation
- Pain point and opportunity register
- Discovery-to-design handover pack

---

## What This Skill Covers

The Discovery Blueprint is the structured process for uncovering a customer's operational pain points, performance gaps, and strategic priorities — before proposing an EaaS solution. It converts customer conversations into a qualified opportunity definition.

---

## Why It Matters in EaaS

EaaS is sold on outcomes, not features. The Discovery Blueprint ensures the sales team understands what outcomes the customer actually cares about (uptime? throughput? cost-per-unit?) before designing a commercial model. Without it, providers design contracts around their own capabilities rather than the customer's needs.

---

## Key Activities

1. **Stakeholder mapping** — Identify all decision-makers, influencers, and end-users across Operations, Finance, Procurement, and Engineering
2. **Pain point interviews** — Structured interviews to surface current performance problems: downtime, quality failures, maintenance costs, energy waste
3. **Baseline performance data collection** — Gather existing KPI data: uptime records, OEE reports, maintenance logs, energy bills
4. **Strategic priority alignment** — Map customer pain points to strategic priorities (cost reduction, sustainability, capacity expansion)
5. **Competitive context scan** — Understand what alternatives the customer is considering (buy outright, lease, different supplier)
6. **Opportunity qualification** — Score the opportunity against EaaS fit criteria: asset intensity, measurability, long-term relationship potential

---

## Discovery Blueprint Template

| Dimension | Questions to Answer |
|-----------|---------------------|
| **Pain** | What keeps the operations manager up at night? What breaks most often? |
| **Measurement** | What KPIs do they track today? How frequently? How reliably? |
| **Economics** | What does downtime cost them per hour? What's their current maintenance spend? |
| **Decision** | Who signs? Who influences? What's their procurement process? |
| **Timeline** | When is the equipment needed? What's the current equipment age / end-of-life? |
| **Risk appetite** | Have they done performance-based contracts before? What was the outcome? |

---

## Key Outputs

- Discovery Brief (2–4 pages): opportunity summary, pain points, baseline KPIs, decision-maker map
- Opportunity Qualification Score
- Recommended next step: proceed to Solution Design (A2) or disqualify
