# A1.4 — Design Blueprint

> **EaaS E2E Operating Model** · Group: A1 · Strategy & Discovery · Role: Solution Designer
> Version: 1.24.0 · License: Apache-2.0 · Author: Luis Prato

**Summary:** Translate discovery findings into a structured EaaS solution concept — defining the outcome model, asset scope, SLA framework, and service wrapper before commercial development begins.

**Key Outcomes:**
- EaaS solution concept document
- Outcome model definition
- Asset scope and SLA framework
- Design-to-deal handover pack

---

## What This Skill Covers

The Design Blueprint translates discovery findings into a structured commercial and technical design for the EaaS offer. It bridges the gap between customer needs (from Discovery) and the full solution design (A2 group), by establishing the commercial architecture, performance commitments, and delivery model at a conceptual level.

---

## Why It Matters in EaaS

EaaS deals fail when a solution is priced before it is designed. The Design Blueprint forces the internal team to agree on the commercial model (what outcome, at what price, with what risk sharing) before entering detailed solution design — avoiding costly redesigns and misaligned proposals.

---

## Key Activities

1. **Commercial model selection** — Choose the EaaS pricing model: pay-per-use, pay-per-outcome, subscription, hybrid
2. **Outcome definition** — Define the primary outcome the customer is buying (e.g., "guaranteed ≥98.5% uptime of Line 4 compressors")
3. **Risk allocation framework** — Identify which risks are borne by the provider vs. customer: performance risk, usage risk, technology obsolescence
4. **Asset ownership structure** — Decide: does the provider own the asset throughout? Is there a purchase option? What happens at end of contract?
5. **Service scope definition** — Define what is included: installation, training, spare parts, consumables, software, field service
6. **Internal feasibility check** — Validate that the proposed design is deliverable given current capabilities, supply chain, and financial constraints

---

## Design Blueprint Output Structure

```
1. Opportunity Summary
2. Proposed Commercial Model
3. Outcome Commitments (draft KPIs)
4. Risk Allocation Matrix
5. Asset Ownership & End-of-Life Terms
6. Included / Excluded Service Scope
7. Internal Feasibility Assessment
8. Recommended Next Steps
```

---

## Key Outputs

- Design Blueprint document (internal)
- Commercial model selection rationale
- Draft outcome commitments for customer alignment
- Go / No-Go recommendation for full solution design
