## What This Skill Covers

Product Assessment evaluates whether a specific product or equipment type is a good candidate for an EaaS / outcome-based commercial model. Not every product is suitable — EaaS works best with assets that have measurable output, stable operating conditions, and predictable maintenance profiles.

---

## Why It Matters in EaaS

Providers sometimes pursue EaaS for products that are fundamentally incompatible with the model — highly variable operating conditions, unmeasurable outputs, or extremely high residual-value uncertainty. Product Assessment prevents the business from committing to outcome guarantees on assets that cannot reliably deliver them.

---

## EaaS Product Suitability Scorecard

| Criterion | Poor Fit | Good Fit | Score (1–5) |
|-----------|----------|----------|-------------|
| **Output measurability** | Output is qualitative, hard to measure | Output is digital, automatically measurable | |
| **Operating condition stability** | Highly variable inputs (seasonal, customer-dependent) | Controlled, predictable operating environment | |
| **Maintenance predictability** | Random failure patterns, high spare parts variability | Known wear patterns, predictable service intervals | |
| **Asset lifetime clarity** | Uncertain economic life; rapid obsolescence | Clear 5–10+ year economic life with known depreciation | |
| **Remote monitoring capability** | No connectivity; no telemetry | Full IoT connectivity; real-time data available | |
| **Performance baseline availability** | No historical performance data | Strong baseline data from existing fleet | |
| **Residual value confidence** | High uncertainty in end-of-contract asset value | Established secondary market; predictable residual value | |

**Score interpretation:** 28–35 = Strong EaaS candidate · 18–27 = Conditional (address gaps first) · <18 = Not suitable without major redesign

---

## Key Activities

1. **Product performance audit** — Review field data from existing installations: uptime, failure rates, MTTR, maintenance cost per unit
2. **Operating condition analysis** — Map the range of customer environments the product operates in; quantify variability
3. **Residual value modelling** — Estimate end-of-contract asset value across scenarios (good condition, average, poor)
4. **Competitive benchmarking** — Compare product reliability and total cost of ownership against alternatives customers could choose
5. **EaaS pricing feasibility** — Validate that the product's performance profile allows competitive EaaS pricing with acceptable margin

---

## Key Outputs

- Product EaaS Suitability Score (with dimension-level breakdown)
- Product Performance Data Summary
- Residual Value Estimate (base case / upside / downside)
- Go / No-Go recommendation for EaaS on this product
