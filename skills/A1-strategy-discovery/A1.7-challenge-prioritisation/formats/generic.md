# A1.7 — Challenge Prioritisation

> **EaaS E2E Operating Model** · Group: A1 · Strategy & Discovery · Role: Strategy Lead
> Version: 1.24.0 · License: Apache-2.0 · Author: Luis Prato

**Summary:** Identify and rank the internal and external barriers to EaaS transition so leadership can allocate resources to the challenges that matter most.

**Key Outcomes:**
- EaaS barrier register with severity ratings
- Prioritised challenge roadmap
- Resource allocation recommendations
- Executive challenge briefing

---

## What This Skill Covers

Challenge Prioritisation ranks the customer's operational, commercial, and technical challenges in order of impact and urgency — so the EaaS solution is designed to address the highest-value problems first, and the contract is structured around the outcomes that matter most to the customer.

---

## Why It Matters in EaaS

Discovery surfaces many pain points. Not all of them can be addressed in a first contract, and attempting to solve everything creates a complex, slow-moving deal. Challenge Prioritisation forces a strategic choice: which two or three outcomes will define the success of this EaaS relationship? These become the primary KPIs in the SLA.

---

## Prioritisation Framework: Impact × Urgency × Measurability

Rate each identified challenge on three dimensions (1–5):

| Dimension | What It Measures |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Business Impact** | What is the financial or strategic cost of this challenge? |
| **Urgency** | How time-sensitive is solving this? Is there a regulatory, competitive, or operational deadline? |
| **Measurability** | How easily can improvement be quantified? Does data exist to verify performance? |

**Priority Score = Impact × Urgency × Measurability**  
Range: 1–125. Top 2–3 challenges (highest score) become the primary outcome objectives.

---

## Common Prioritised Challenges in EaaS

- **Unplanned downtime** — typically highest impact; often most urgent for production-critical assets
- **Energy cost per unit** — increasingly urgent with energy price volatility and ESG commitments
- **Quality defect rate** — high impact in regulated industries (food, pharma, automotive)
- **Maintenance cost predictability** — a CFO priority; makes EaaS budgeting more attractive than CapEx
- **Capacity constraints** — urgency driven by market demand growth; measurable via throughput KPIs

---

## Key Activities

1. **Challenge inventory** — Compile all pain points identified during discovery interviews
2. **Impact quantification** — Where possible, put a number on each challenge (cost per hour of downtime, €/unit of scrap, etc.)
3. **Scoring workshop** — Facilitate a joint customer–provider session to score challenges using the framework
4. **Priority list** — Rank challenges; select the top 2–3 as primary outcome objectives
5. **Out-of-scope list** — Explicitly document challenges not addressed in this contract phase; plan for Phase 2

---

## Key Outputs

- Prioritised Challenge Register (scored matrix)
- Selected Outcome Objectives (top 2–3)
- Rationale for in-scope / out-of-scope decisions
- Input to SLA Design (A3.7) and Business Case (A3.1)
