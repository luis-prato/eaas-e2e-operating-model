## What This Skill Covers

The Tendering Procedure covers the end-to-end process of responding to a formal customer tender (RFP/RFQ/ITT) for an EaaS solution — from tender analysis through bid strategy, proposal development, and submission. It addresses the unique challenges of tendering for outcome-based contracts.

---

## Why It Matters in EaaS

Most procurement processes are designed for product purchases, not outcome-based services. Providers that apply traditional bid responses to EaaS tenders lose — either by under-promising (conservative, low-winning rate) or over-promising (high-winning rate, catastrophic delivery). The Tendering Procedure adapts the bid process to EaaS requirements.

---

## EaaS Tender Analysis Framework

Before writing a single word of a proposal, analyse the tender document:

### 1. Red Flags (consider walking away)
- Evaluation criteria weighted purely on price (<30% for non-price criteria)
- Payment terms that require performance upfront without milestone payments
- Outcome definitions that are vague or unmeasurable
- No provision for baseline measurement before SLA commencement
- Unlimited liability clauses with no cap
- Force majeure carve-outs absent or inadequate

### 2. EaaS-Specific Evaluation Criteria (look for / advocate for)
- Technical capability and track record (40%+)
- Service model and response time commitments (20%+)
- SLA design quality and feasibility (20%+)
- Commercial creativity and risk-sharing (10%+)
- Price (10–20%)

## Tendering Process Steps

1. **Tender receipt & qualification** — Apply Go/No-Go checklist; allocate bid resources
2. **Tender analysis** — Identify must-wins, differentiators, compliance requirements, and risks
3. **Win strategy session** — Define the bid's unique value proposition and competitive positioning
4. **Clarification questions** — Submit written questions to clarify ambiguous requirements (especially KPI definitions and measurement methods)
5. **Proposal development** — Build the proposal across: Executive Summary, Technical Solution, Service Model, SLA Design, Commercial Offer, Risk Management, Track Record
6. **Internal review** — Legal, Finance, Operations, and Leadership sign-off on commitments and risk
7. **Submission** — Meet all format and deadline requirements; confirm receipt
8. **Clarification stage** — Respond to customer questions professionally and consistently
9. **Negotiation** — Engage on commercial terms; maintain business case viability red lines
10. **Contract award** — Transition to Implementation Blueprint (A2.5)

---

## EaaS Proposal Structure

```
1. Executive Summary (1–2 pages): The outcome we guarantee and why we are uniquely positioned to deliver it
2. Understanding of Requirements: Mirror the customer's objectives back to them
3. Proposed EaaS Solution: Asset description, technical specification, service scope
4. Service Model: How we deliver outcomes — response times, monitoring, maintenance
5. SLA Design: KPIs, targets, measurement method, payment logic, remedies
6. Commercial Offer: Pricing model, fee structure, escalation, payment terms
7. Risk Management: How we manage and share risk; force majeure; insurance
8. Track Record: Reference contracts; performance data from comparable deployments
9. Implementation Plan: How we transition from award to go-live
10. Appendices: Certificates, financial statements, team CVs
```
