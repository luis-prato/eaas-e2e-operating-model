## What This Skill Covers

Internal Organisation defines the governance structure, team design, and operating model required to run EaaS contracts at scale — covering roles, decision authorities, cross-functional workflows, and the internal handoffs that keep outcome delivery consistent across the contract lifecycle.

---

## Why It Matters in EaaS

EaaS is not a product sale followed by a service ticket. It is a long-term service relationship that requires sustained coordination between Sales, Finance, Operations, Legal, and Customer Success. Without a clear internal model, accountability diffuses — and the customer experiences inconsistency, missed SLAs, and eroded trust.

## EaaS Organisational Model

### Core EaaS Functions

| Function | Role in EaaS | Reports To |
|----------|-------------|------------|
| **EaaS Commercial** | Bid strategy, pricing, contract design | Sales / BD Director |
| **EaaS Operations** | Service delivery, SLA management, field teams | Operations Director |
| **Customer Success** | Performance reviews, escalation management, renewals | Commercial / Ops |
| **Finance** | Revenue recognition, asset financing, margin tracking | CFO |
| **Legal** | Contract drafting, risk review, compliance | General Counsel |
| **Technology / IoT** | Remote monitoring platform, data, analytics | CTO / IT Director |

## Contract Governance Structure

Each live EaaS contract should have a defined governance structure:

### Level 1 — Operational (Monthly)
- **Participants:** Provider Contract Manager + Customer Operations Lead
- **Agenda:** KPI review, open issues, maintenance schedule, invoicing
- **Output:** Performance dashboard sign-off; escalation log

### Level 2 — Commercial (Quarterly)
- **Participants:** Provider Account Director + Customer Procurement / Finance
- **Agenda:** Business review, change request log, benchmarking, relationship health
- **Output:** QBR report; action register

### Level 3 — Strategic (Annual or at renewal)
- **Participants:** Provider Senior Leadership + Customer C-Suite
- **Agenda:** Contract performance vs. original business case; renewal terms; expansion
- **Output:** Strategic account plan; renewal heads of terms

---

## Key Roles

### Contract Manager (Provider side)
- Day-to-day SLA performance management
- Coordinates field service and customer communications
- Owns the performance dashboard and monthly report

### Customer Success Manager (Provider side)
- Owns the customer relationship at operational level
- Manages escalations and QBR delivery
- Identifies expansion and renewal opportunities

### EaaS Commercial Manager
- Owns the contract business case and margin tracking
- Manages change requests with financial impact
- Supports renewal negotiation

## RACI for EaaS Contract Governance

| Activity | Commercial | Operations | Customer Success | Finance | Legal |
|----------|------------|------------|-----------------|---------|-------|
| Contract design | A/R | C | I | C | R |
| SLA monitoring | I | R/A | C | I | I |
| Monthly reporting | I | R | A | I | I |
| Invoicing | C | I | I | R/A | I |
| Change request | A | C | R | C | R |
| Escalation management | C | C | R/A | I | C |
| Renewal | A/R | C | R | C | C |

R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed

---

## Scaling EaaS Operations

As the portfolio of EaaS contracts grows, the provider must shift from ad-hoc management to a structured portfolio model:

- **1–5 contracts:** Managed within existing teams with named leads
- **5–20 contracts:** Dedicated EaaS team; shared Contract Manager pool
- **20+ contracts:** EaaS Centre of Excellence; standardised playbooks; tiered account management
