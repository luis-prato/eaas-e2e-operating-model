## What This Skill Covers

Customer Onboarding manages the human, process, and system-side transition that turns a signed EaaS contract into a functioning service relationship. It covers stakeholder introductions, process alignment, system access provisioning, and the first 90-day experience that defines whether the customer trusts the provider to deliver.

---

## Why It Matters in EaaS

A technically perfect installation can still fail commercially if the customer doesn't know how to use the service, who to call, or what to expect. Onboarding shapes the customer's mental model of the EaaS relationship — and poor onboarding is the leading cause of early escalations, SLA disputes, and relationship breakdown in the first contract year.

## Onboarding Journey

### Week 1: Introduction & Alignment
- Customer kick-off meeting: introduce the delivery team (Contract Manager, Customer Success Manager, Field Service lead)
- Share the service delivery handbook: how to raise issues, escalation path, reporting schedule
- Confirm customer contacts and communication preferences
- Review the implementation plan together; confirm key milestones and customer dependencies

### Weeks 2–4: System & Process Setup
- Provision customer access to the monitoring dashboard / portal
- Walkthrough of real-time performance data and how to interpret KPIs
- Agree the format and frequency of performance reports
- Confirm the maintenance schedule and customer-side preparation requirements
- Set up invoicing contact and billing process

### Months 2–3: Go-Live & Stabilisation
- First performance review: walkthrough of initial KPI data
- Resolve any teething issues before they become patterns
- Confirm operator training is complete and effective
- 30-day check-in call: how is the service meeting expectations?
- 90-day review: formal assessment against baseline KPIs; first QBR preparation

## Onboarding Checklist

**People**
- [ ] Customer contacts confirmed and documented (operational, commercial, technical, escalation)
- [ ] Provider team introduced (Contract Manager, CSM, field lead, emergency contact)
- [ ] Escalation path communicated and acknowledged

**Process**
- [ ] Service delivery handbook distributed and reviewed
- [ ] Maintenance schedule agreed and confirmed with site operations
- [ ] Reporting cadence and format confirmed
- [ ] Invoice process set up (PO number, contact, approval chain)

**Systems**
- [ ] Customer portal access provisioned and tested
- [ ] KPI dashboard walkthrough completed
- [ ] Performance alert thresholds configured per customer preference
- [ ] IoT telemetry validated and live in customer-visible view

---

## Onboarding Experience Principles

**Reduce surprises.** The customer signed up for outcomes, not complexity. Every interaction should reduce uncertainty, not add to it.

**Make the invisible visible.** One of the hardest things about EaaS is that great service delivery is invisible — the machine just works. The onboarding period is when you show customers the work behind the reliability: the monitoring, the maintenance visits, the data.

**Set the right rhythm.** The cadence established in onboarding becomes the norm. Set it too infrequently and small issues compound. Set it right and you build a relationship that makes renewals automatic.
