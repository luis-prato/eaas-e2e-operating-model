# A4.3 — Customer Success

> **EaaS E2E Operating Model** · Group: A4 · Operations & Capability · Role: Customer Success Manager
> Version: 1.24.0 · License: Apache-2.0 · Author: Luis Prato

**Summary:** Manage the ongoing customer relationship to maximise satisfaction, prevent churn, and build the commercial conditions for contract renewal and expansion.

**Key Outcomes:**
- Account health scoring framework
- Customer engagement cadence (monthly/quarterly/annual)
- Value communication and reporting toolkit
- Renewal playbook with 18-month timeline

---

## What This Skill Covers

Customer Success manages the ongoing health of the EaaS relationship — from performance monitoring and relationship governance through issue escalation, value communication, and renewal positioning. It is the commercial and relational engine that keeps the customer renewing and expanding.

---

## Why It Matters in EaaS

EaaS revenue is recurring. Renewal is not automatic — it is earned every quarter through consistent performance, proactive communication, and demonstrated value. Customer Success is the function that converts operational delivery into commercial loyalty. Without it, even technically excellent service delivery can fail to renew because the customer never understood the value being created.

## Customer Success Operating Model

### Account Health Framework

Score each account monthly across three dimensions:

| Dimension | Green | Amber | Red |
|-----------|-------|-------|-----|
| **SLA Performance** | ≥ target KPI | 90–99% of target | < 90% of target |
| **Relationship Health** | Proactive, engaged, expanding | Passive, no concerns | Escalating, disengaged, or competitive review underway |
| **Commercial Health** | Invoiced on time, no disputes | Minor payment delay | Dispute in progress or overdue balance |

---

## Customer Success Cadence

### Monthly (Contract Manager)
- Pull SLA performance data from monitoring platform
- Generate monthly performance report
- Send report to customer; address any questions
- Log open issues and track to resolution

### Quarterly (Customer Success Manager)
- Quarterly Business Review (QBR): performance vs. contract; value delivered; roadmap
- Relationship check: stakeholder mapping, health score review
- Commercial review: invoicing status, change requests, benchmarking
- Renewal positioning: update renewal timeline; identify expansion opportunities

### Annual (Account Director + Senior Customer Contact)
- Strategic account review: outcomes vs. original business case
- Contract renewal or extension negotiation
- Identify new sites, asset types, or service scope for expansion
- Executive relationship investment

---

## Value Communication

EaaS customers often undervalue the service because they don't see what they're getting. Customer Success must make value explicit:

- **Uptime delivered** vs. historical baseline (before EaaS)
- **Unplanned downtime prevented** (incidents caught by monitoring before failure)
- **Cost avoided** (what CapEx + maintenance would have cost under the old model)
- **ESG outcomes** (energy saved, emissions reduced, circular economy contribution)
- **Innovation delivered** (technology upgrades, process improvements made during contract)

---

## Escalation Management

When an issue is raised by the customer:

1. Acknowledge within 4 hours (or per contracted response time)
2. Classify severity: P1 (production impact), P2 (performance degraded), P3 (administrative)
3. Assign owner and communicate resolution timeline
4. Update customer every 24 hours until resolved
5. Root cause analysis for P1/P2; preventive action documented
6. Close issue formally with customer sign-off

## Renewal Playbook

| Timeline | Action |
|----------|--------|
| 18 months before expiry | Internal renewal strategy review; account health assessment |
| 12 months before expiry | Customer conversation: satisfaction, needs, priorities |
| 9 months before expiry | Commercial proposal drafted; expansion options identified |
| 6 months before expiry | Formal renewal negotiation begins |
| 3 months before expiry | Heads of terms agreed; legal drafting commenced |
| 30 days before expiry | Contract signed; implementation of any new terms confirmed |
