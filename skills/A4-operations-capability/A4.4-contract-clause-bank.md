## What This Skill Covers

The Contract Clause Bank is a structured library of pre-approved, legally reviewed contract clauses specific to EaaS arrangements — covering SLA design, risk allocation, liability, IP ownership, data rights, force majeure, and termination. It accelerates contract drafting while maintaining commercial and legal discipline.

---

## Why It Matters in EaaS

EaaS contracts are complex and non-standard. Without a clause bank, every deal is negotiated from scratch — slow, expensive, and inconsistent. Legal teams re-review familiar ground, commercial teams accept suboptimal positions under time pressure, and clause variations across contracts create operational compliance nightmares. A clause bank is how EaaS providers industrialise their contracting process.

## Clause Bank Structure

### Module 1: Definitions
- **Outcome / KPI definitions** — Availability, Uptime, Performance Index, Response Time
- **Measurement period** — How SLA periods are defined and calculated
- **Asset definitions** — Equipment, Installed Base, Spare Parts, Consumables
- **Service definitions** — Planned Maintenance, Reactive Maintenance, Emergency Response

### Module 2: SLA & Performance
- **KPI target clauses** — Baseline, target, stretch performance levels
- **Measurement methodology** — Data source, calculation method, dispute resolution
- **SLA carve-outs** — Events that suspend SLA obligations (force majeure, customer-caused, planned downtime)
- **Payment linkage** — How performance below/above target affects the periodic fee
- **Remedy mechanism** — Service credits, re-performance obligations, termination triggers

### Module 3: Asset Ownership & Risk
- **Title retention** — Provider retains title to equipment throughout contract
- **Risk of loss** — Who bears the risk if the asset is damaged, destroyed, or stolen
- **Insurance obligations** — Minimum coverage levels; evidence requirements
- **Residual value** — How residual value is handled at end of contract

### Module 4: Liability & Indemnity
- **Liability cap** — Maximum aggregate liability (typically linked to annual contract value)
- **Consequential loss exclusion** — Standard exclusion of indirect/consequential losses
- **IP indemnity** — Provider indemnifies customer against third-party IP claims
- **Data breach indemnity** — Allocation of liability for IoT/data security incidents

### Module 5: Data & Technology
- **Data ownership** — Operational data generated by the asset (who owns it; who can use it)
- **Data security obligations** — Encryption, access controls, breach notification
- **Platform availability** — Uptime obligations for monitoring/reporting platforms
- **Change of platform** — Rights on technology platform replacement or upgrade

### Module 6: Force Majeure & Change
- **Force majeure definition** — Qualifying events; notification requirements; mutual rights
- **Change in law** — Who bears cost of regulatory change affecting performance
- **Material adverse change** — Circumstances triggering renegotiation rights
- **Indexation mechanism** — Annual adjustment formula; index selection; caps and floors

### Module 7: Term & Termination
- **Contract term** — Initial term; renewal mechanism; notice period
- **Termination for cause** — Trigger events; cure periods; consequences
- **Termination for convenience** — Early termination fee formula
- **Step-in rights** — Customer's right to step in if provider fails to deliver
- **Exit / transition obligations** — Asset recovery, data export, knowledge transfer at end of contract

## Clause Governance

Each clause in the bank should carry:
- **Approval status:** Approved / Under Review / Deprecated
- **Legal owner:** Named lawyer who approved the clause
- **Version:** With change log
- **Usage note:** Standard / Negotiable / Non-negotiable / EaaS-specific
- **Linked clauses:** Other clauses that must be used together
