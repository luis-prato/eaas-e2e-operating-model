## What This Skill Covers

Return & Refurbishment covers the end-of-contract asset lifecycle — from equipment retrieval at contract expiry through condition assessment, refurbishment, residual value realisation, and redeployment or responsible disposal. It is the circular economy closing loop of the EaaS model.

---

## Why It Matters in EaaS

The provider retains ownership of EaaS assets throughout the contract. What happens to those assets at contract end determines whether the residual value built into the original business case is actually recovered — or written off. A well-managed return and refurbishment process extends asset life, generates secondary revenue, reduces CapEx on the next contract, and delivers ESG outcomes that are increasingly valued by customers and investors.

---

## End-of-Contract Asset Lifecycle

### Phase 1: Contract Expiry Planning (6–12 months before end)
- Review asset condition data from the monitoring platform (age, usage hours, maintenance history)
- Assess residual value against the original business case assumption
- Determine asset disposition strategy: redeploy (same customer, new site), reconfigure (different customer or use case), refurbish and resell, or dismantle and recover materials
- Notify customer of return process and schedule per contract terms
- Confirm insurance coverage through retrieval and transport

### Phase 2: Asset Return
- Schedule asset retrieval with customer (access, logistics, specialist lifting if required)
- De-install: remove IoT sensors, recover spare parts, disconnect utilities
- Condition inspection at de-installation: photograph, document faults, compare to baseline
- Transport to refurbishment facility or depot

### Phase 3: Condition Assessment
- Full technical inspection against original specification
- Graded condition report:
  - **Grade A:** Minimal wear; suitable for direct redeployment
  - **Grade B:** Moderate wear; refurbishment required for redeployment
  - **Grade C:** Significant wear; major overhaul required or component recovery only
  - **Grade D:** End of economic life; materials recovery only

### Phase 4: Refurbishment
Scope varies by condition grade:

| Grade | Typical Refurbishment Scope |
|-------|-----------------------------|
| A | Clean, calibrate, update firmware, replace consumables |
| B | Replace wear parts, repaint/refurbish enclosure, recommission |
| C | Major component overhaul or replacement; structural inspection; full recommission |
| D | Disassemble; recover reusable components; recycle materials per WEEE/REACH |

### Phase 5: Residual Value Realisation
- **Internal redeployment:** Asset goes directly into the next EaaS contract — no CapEx on that deal
- **Secondary market sale:** Asset sold to third party (refurbished equipment market, export market)
- **Leaseback:** Asset sold to a financial institution and leased back for the next contract
- **Parts recovery:** High-value components (motors, electronics, sensors) recovered and inventoried as spare parts

---

## Residual Value Management

The residual value assumption is built into the original business case. Managing it requires:

- **Condition-based maintenance** during the contract to preserve residual value
- **Asset tracking** throughout contract life (usage hours, maintenance history, incident log)
- **Residual value benchmarking** against secondary market prices annually
- **Provisioning adjustment** if residual value assumption changes materially mid-contract

---

## Circular Economy Reporting

Return & refurbishment generates ESG-reportable outcomes:

- **Assets refurbished and redeployed** (units; % of returned fleet)
- **Materials recovered** (tonnes; material type)
- **Avoided manufacturing emissions** (CO₂e equivalent of refurbishment vs. new production)
- **Landfill waste avoided** (tonnes)
- **Components reused as spare parts** (units; value)

---

## Key Outputs

- End-of-Contract Asset Disposition Plan (per contract)
- Condition Assessment Report (per returned asset)
- Refurbishment Work Order and Completion Report
- Residual Value Realisation Report (vs. business case assumption)
- Circular Economy Impact Report (ESG metrics from the return and refurbishment programme)
- Secondary Asset Inventory (refurbished assets available for next EaaS contract)
